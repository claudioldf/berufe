# Legitimate-interest assessment: referred professional profiles

## Processing assessed

An identity-verified Berufe professional may refer another adult professional using a name,
Brazilian mobile phone, professional services, coverage area, and an optional relationship
context. A basic profile can be publicly discoverable, is labeled as created by referral, and
can be claimed through SMS verification or opposed through Support.

Controller: Rising Consultoria de Software LTDA., CNPJ 36.443.360/0001-05. Assessment date:
23 August 2026. Public-document version: `1.0`.

## Purpose and benefit

The purpose is to make genuine local professional supply discoverable and to represent an
existing professional connection. The expected benefit is relevant local discovery for
visitors and a low-friction way for a referred professional to claim and complete a profile.
The data must not be reused for advertising lists, sale of contacts, unsolicited campaigns,
or unrelated scoring.

## Necessity

The flow uses the smallest practical launch set: display name, one Brazilian mobile number,
optional service/coverage, relationship type, and optional short context. The phone is not
rendered as text on the public page; it is used to build a WhatsApp action and to let the
holder claim the profile by OTP. Publication requires an attestation by the referrer and an
active referral. Birthdate, identity files, customer data, e-mail, and private account data
are not collected for the referred profile.

Requiring the referred person to register before any profile exists would remove the core
referral benefit. Publishing less than the basic professional facts would not support useful
discovery or distinguish the referred person. These conclusions must be revisited if Berufe
adds proactive messaging, monetization, rankings, enrichment, or new data sources.

## Reasonable expectations and impact

A professional may reasonably expect a trusted colleague to share limited work contact data
for a professional referral, but may not expect a public, search-engine-visible profile.
Public visibility therefore creates a meaningful risk of unwanted exposure, stale or
incorrect information, impersonation, spam, and loss of control. The absence of proactive
notice before first access increases that risk and makes clear on-page origin, simple
opposition, and rapid response essential.

The assessment does not justify sensitive data, automated adverse decisions, secret ratings,
bulk export, unrelated outreach, indefinite publication without an active basis, or profiles
for anyone known to be under 18.

## Safeguards and balancing conclusion

The launch flow is acceptable only while all safeguards below remain effective:

- only a registered, phone-verified, identity-verified professional can create the referral;
- the referrer expressly attests authority to share the professional contact;
- the profile and relationship disclose their referred and unclaimed status;
- public fields are minimized and the phone is not printed on the page;
- the phone holder can claim the profile through SMS;
- correction, immediate unpublication, opposition, and eligible erasure are available through
  `suporte@berufe.com.br`;
- suspension immediately removes the profile from public search, profile, quote, portfolio,
  and recommendation output;
- rate limits, post-publication moderation, log filtering, access control, and retention jobs
  reduce misuse and unnecessary persistence;
- the controller records active referrals and deletes them in a verified erasure workflow.

With those limits, the controller's purpose can provisionally outweigh the impact for the MVP.
If opposition cannot be handled promptly, abuse becomes material, or any safeguard is removed,
publication of new referred profiles must be disabled until a new assessment is approved.

This assessment must be reviewed by counsel qualified in Brazilian privacy law before launch
and at least annually, as well as after a material change to data, purpose, visibility,
messaging, ranking, geography, provider, or rights workflow.
